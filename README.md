When you have multiple monitors with different sizes and resolutions, sometimes it can be difficult to get them all aligned nicely and mapping mouse coordinates across them. This is especially tricky if you want to have a single graphic spread across all screens like one giant wallpaper image. The goal is to create a system settings panel that's similar to the existing controls, but with extended capabilities. We want to be able to indicate where your screens are in physical space, what their actual physical dimensions are vs the resolution (pixel density) and have visual tools for aligning them. For example, creating a virtual real-world space and projecting lines out of one monitor indicating the top, bottom, and sides. Then on your other displays, you will be able to grab those lines and drag them like guides until they correctly line up with with the other screens. Another tool would be to take a straight-on photo of your display setup, mark out (or autodetect) the corners of your displays, and have all the display settings rectified to where they are in relation and scale to each other.
